Screens to read barcodes on stock operations with a barcode scanner (USB or Bluetooth scanners typing the barcode followed by Enter) or typing them, on desktop and mobile browsers: receipts, deliveries, internal transfers, relocations, batch transfers, unplanned transfers and inventory counts. No Odoo Enterprise license is required.

All the logic lives in `stock_capture`, shared with the other capture screens (e.g. the weighing screens of `stock_weighing`); this module adds the screens, the barcode main menu and the buttons to open them.
