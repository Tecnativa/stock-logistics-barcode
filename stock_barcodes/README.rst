Screens to read barcodes on stock operations with a barcode scanner (USB
or Bluetooth scanners typing the barcode followed by Enter) or typing
them, on desktop and mobile browsers: receipts, deliveries, internal
transfers, relocations, batch transfers, unplanned transfers and
inventory counts. No Odoo Enterprise license is required.

All the logic lives in ``stock_capture``, shared with the other capture
screens (e.g. the weighing screens of ``stock_weighing``); this module
adds the screens, the barcode main menu and the buttons to open them.

Configuration
=============

Before configuring, analyze with the people doing them the flow of each
operation: what is read and in which order, what the screen must propose
and what it must refuse. Then adjust a capture profile to each flow
(*Inventory > Configuration > Capture Profiles*) and set it on the
operation types, as explained in ``stock_capture``.

Barcodes on master data:

- Products: *Barcode* of the product.
- Locations: *Barcode* of the location (multi-location setting).
- Packagings: barcode of a unit of the product (several units of measure
  setting), e.g. *Pack of 6*.
- Lots, serial numbers and packages: their name is their barcode.

The tiles of the *Barcodes* menu are barcode actions (*Inventory >
Configuration > Barcode Actions*): the window action or method they
open, the records they count and the barcode that opens them from the
menu. Select them and use *Print barcodes* to get a sheet with their
Code128 barcodes.

Usage
=====

Open the barcode screen from:

1. The *Barcodes* menu: choose a tile (Receipts, Deliveries, Internal
   Transfers, Inventory, Operations) or read the barcode of a tile.
2. The cards of *Inventory > Overview*: read on any ready transfer of
   the operation type, list its transfers ready to read or start a new
   unplanned transfer.
3. The *Scan* button of a transfer, a batch transfer or, with
   ``stock_barcodes_mrp``, a manufacturing order.

The screen keeps what you read until it is captured, and the session
survives the navigation for two days.

**Reading.** Read in any order (free mode) or follow the pending card
proposed (guided mode). A product adds one unit (a packaging, its
quantity; a weight or GS1 barcode, its quantity); a tracked product
waits for its lot unless the profile takes it from the stock; a location
sets the source (or the destination, depending on the step); a package
with stock moves with all its content, another package becomes the
destination package. The message tells what was read and the line below
what the screen waits for. Readings that cannot be captured play an
error sound and say why: wrong product, lot or location for the card,
product not demanded, quantity not available in the location, or over
the demand (*Capture anyway* captures it).

**Manual entry.** The keyboard button makes the fields editable;
*Confirm* captures them. With manual confirmation in the profile every
reading waits for *Confirm*, and repeated readings can add up.

**Cards.** The pending operations or detailed operations of the
document, sorted by location. Tap a card to fill the screen from it. Its
buttons capture what is left, undo what was captured, leave a detailed
operation without capturing it or close an operation with or without
backorder. The eye button switches between pending and captured cards
and the list button shows the captured detailed operations, which can be
removed.

**Other tools.** Product information (stock by location, on hand,
incoming, outgoing, forecasted) and selection of a source or destination
location with stock; new destination package and keeping it for the next
readings; serial numbers entered in the detailed operations of the
product; registering the barcode of a new packaging.

**Validate** validates the document with the captured quantities,
putting it in a package first when the profile asks for it, and goes
back to the transfers ready to read.

**Inventory.** The *Inventory* tile counts the stock of the main
warehouse. Each reading adds to the counted quantity of the location
read (or replaces it, depending on the profile); serial numbers count
one unit each. Cards show what you counted or what is pending, with
buttons to add, remove or clear. *Apply* applies your counts with the
reason Odoo asks for.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Reading with the camera of the device (``BarcodeView`` of Odoo 20.0).
- The sound and notification settings per user.
- 18.0 grouped the pending cards by any expression; 20.0 groups detailed
  operations by location and product only.

Changelog
=========

20.0.1.0.0 (2026-10-17)
-----------------------

- [MIG] Rebuilt on ``stock_capture``: the option groups become capture
  profiles (migrated with their steps; fields of other modules are
  migrated by them), the reading logic and the documents (transfers,
  operation types, batch transfers, inventories) move to the engine, and
  the screens read the real operations instead of copies. Batch
  transfers, part of ``stock`` in 20.0, are read without
  ``stock_barcodes_picking_batch``.
- [MIG] Quantities read on pending operations (``qty_picked``) and the
  backorder decisions are captured when migrating.

18.0.1.6.0 (2026-10-02)
-----------------------

- [ADD] Product information: a button next to the read product opens its
  on hand, incoming, outgoing and forecasted quantities and its stock in
  each location, ordered by the removal strategy (reversed on
  receptions). Tapping a location fills the screen location.
- [ADD] Buttons next to the source and destination locations list only
  the locations with stock to pick one, the destination ones in reverse
  order. On small screens the field icons replace these buttons.

18.0.1.5.0 (2026-09-29)
-----------------------

- [IMP] *Replace quantity on each reading* (``no_increase_qty_done``)
  now also applies to inventories: a reading is added to the counted
  quantity unless it is set. *Accumulate read quantity* is renamed to
  *Accumulate repeated readings* and adds one unit (or packaging) when
  the same item is read again before a manual confirmation. The
  migration keeps the inventory behavior of each database and leaves the
  new accumulation disabled.
- [FIX] Confirming a counted inventory line edited from the list added
  its quantity again (doubling it) when readings were added; the edited
  line now always replaces its counted quantity.
- [FIX] Map the 15.0 ``move_lines`` value of *Source pending moves* to
  ``move_ids``.
- [REM] Remove the unused *Show all quantities* and *Allow direct
  partial delivery* options.

18.0.1.3.0 (2026-07-08)
-----------------------

- [FIX] Restore bus notifications broken by the 18.0 bus API: scanning
  an action barcode from the main menu (whose client-side scanner
  capture was never wired since the feature was introduced in 16.0), the
  "transfer validated" toast, the manual-edit toggle from a pending line
  and the ``display_notification()`` toasts (e.g. "Lot is required"),
  which were sent with a per-record notification type nobody subscribes
  to. Events are now sent to the scanning user only instead of a
  broadcast channel.
- [FIX] Notify only the most significant message per interaction.
  Reading a barcode triggers sequential lookups and checks that buffer
  intermediate messages (e.g. "Manual entry OK" before the availability
  check rejects the entry), and each one used to raise its own
  notification. Messages are now buffered on the wizard (last one wins,
  as the on-screen message field always behaved) and a single
  notification is sent when the read or the manual confirmation ends:
  always for errors, only in manual entry for informative messages, and
  for "Manual entry OK" only when the whole confirmation succeeded.
- [FIX] Assign serial numbers button: the core removed the
  stock.assign.serial wizard in 18.0 leaving
  stock.move.action_assign_serial() broken (missing
  stock.act_assign_serial_numbers action), so the button crashed. It now
  opens the detailed operations dialog of the pending move, where the
  core 18.0 generate-serials widget lives. Lines receiving a lot/serial
  in that dialog are counted as scanned and linked to the pending card,
  so generated serials show up on the barcode screen.
- [FIX] Show each barcode notification only once. The barcode views
  subscribed the same handler to two bus notification types, which leaks
  a listener on every render (the bus service maps subscriptions by
  callback), so each notification was displayed more and more times as
  the session went on.
- [FIX] Barcode action tile counters: the model-field check never
  matched (checked attributes on a string), so tiles counted all records
  of the model; named filters now count records matching the filter.
- [REF] Drop the ``python-barcode`` external dependency introduced by
  the 17.0 migration: barcode action images are rendered again with the
  core ``ir.actions.report.barcode()`` helper, as in 16.0.
- [REF] Remove pre-17.0 immediate-transfer leftovers, use
  ``_read_group`` aggregation, convert the report model to
  ``AbstractModel``, drop a duplicated ACL, replace deprecated ``t-esc``
  with ``t-out`` and add ``limit=1`` to singleton barcode lookups.
- [REF] Rename misspelled methods (``_set_message_info``,
  ``check_location_condition``, ``check_lot_condition``). The old names
  are kept as deprecated aliases; modules overriding them must move
  their override to the new names.

18.0.1.1.0 (2026-06-22)
-----------------------

- [ADD] New option *Show fixed dest. location* on barcode option groups.
  It shows, in the pending moves list, the destination location already
  planned on the stock move line (for a fixed putaway, destination
  without storage category), and makes a scan reuse that move line
  keeping its destination instead of duplicating it at the generic
  picking destination. It never recomputes the putaway strategy.

13.0.1.1.1 (2021-02-06)
