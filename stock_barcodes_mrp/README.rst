A button on manufacturing orders opens them in the barcode reading
screen of ``stock_barcodes``. Components, the product to manufacture and
byproducts are captured as ``mrp_capture`` does.

.. IMPORTANT::
   This is an alpha version, the data model and design can change at any time without warning.
   Only for development or testing purpose, do not use in production.
   `More details on development status <https://odoo-community.org/page/development-status>`_

Usage
=====

Open a confirmed manufacturing order and press *Scan*. Read the
components to consume, the product to manufacture (with its lot or
package) and the byproducts, then validate to mark the order as done.
