A user preparing a batch transfer on the barcode screen can ask another user to review it (*Request Review*): the batch is left unassigned and listed in the *Batches to Review* tile of the barcode menu and with a *Review* button on the batch.

The reviewer opens the batch in review mode: the cards are the detailed operations captured by the picker, and reading a product (with its lot or package) or confirming a card marks them as reviewed, without changing the captured quantities.
